# Backlog disposition - 48-Hour Verified Systems Audit

**Decision date:** 2026-07-28  
**Disposition:** BACKLOG / CAVE  
**Standalone viability:** Near zero, per owner direction

## Decision

The 48-Hour Verified Systems Audit is not an active standalone product or launch priority.

Its useful material remains absorbed into Mission GroundWork as internal delivery collateral:

- intake and acceptance framing;
- systems-verification checklist concepts;
- handoff structure;
- evidence and acceptance language;
- possible supporting material for future consulting engagements.

## Prohibited next work

Do not:

- create a separate brand;
- build a new website or funnel;
- publish it as a primary product;
- create additional trackers;
- conduct new market research without a live client use case;
- present the prior proposed price as validated demand.

## Reactivation gate

Reactivate only when a current Mission GroundWork prospect explicitly needs a short systems audit and the existing materials can be reused with no more than one bounded revision cycle.

## Source of truth
